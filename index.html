Organisations are usually offered a choice: social consultants who don't understand the data, or tech firms that don't understand the legal and cultural realities of public service. Lewis & Klein is the middle ground — researchers, data scientists, and systems architects working to one standard: evidence-led, ethically transparent, built around your organisation rather than a template.
